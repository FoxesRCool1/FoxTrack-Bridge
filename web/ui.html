Bambu Cloud Experimental

Off

This feature is experimental. Link your Bambu account to add printers that are not in LAN mode. Cloud printers report status and can toggle the light; every other control needs LAN mode. The Bridge keeps one connection for the whole account and backs off slowly after errors, so the account stays inside Bambu's limits.

The password is used once to sign in and is never stored.

Use this when sign-in is blocked. A token lasts about 90 days and cannot be renewed; sign in again when it expires.

Assistant Experimental

Ask questions about your printers, get help connecting one, and work out why a print went wrong. You supply the AI provider and pay for it; the Bridge ships with none. Requests go from this machine straight to the provider and never through FoxTrack. The assistant can only read — it cannot change a setting or control a print.

The OpenAI-compatible endpoint root, ending in /v1. For Ollama that is usually http://localhost:11434/v1.

Stored on this machine in config.json, which is readable only by your user. Leave blank to keep the saved key.

Let the assistant look at printer cameras

Needed to diagnose how a print actually looks. With a cloud provider this sends a photograph of your printer to that provider. With a local model it stays on this machine.

The dashboard has no login. Anyone who can reach it on your network can use the assistant and spend credit on this key.
